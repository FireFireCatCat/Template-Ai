## Verdict
yes. The zip is AGU's own author kit (agu.org), so both classes are published by the rubric's official-kit ruling, and each sample only chooses class options and calls the class's own vocabulary. The preambles add only published packages (`url`, `lineno`, `soul`, `graphicx`) and `trackchanges`, which I judge published (public project with its own documentation, unmodified upstream file, recommended by the kit) and which maps to `draft.todo`. No sample defines a macro, environment, counter or drawing of its own, and nothing needs a tool outside TeX except ordinary BibTeX.
Sensitivity: the classes are hand-written (own `\@startsection`, `\@makecaption`, `\@array`, `$$ \halign` equation and output-routine code). If a reader refuses the official-kit ruling, or refuses the `trackchanges` judgement, this becomes `no` with `BESPOKE/class` or `BESPOKE/package`. The `yes` rests on those two rulings only.

## Content-opaque
none. The example figures, tables and equations are commented-out placeholders with no artwork. Text after `\end{document}` (JT:429-570, ST:369-529) is advice that TeX never reads. Placeholder values (`=Authors=`, `=enter title here=`, `\bibliography{ enter your bibtex bibliography filename here }`) are content.
