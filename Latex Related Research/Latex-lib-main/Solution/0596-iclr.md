## Verdict
yes. The look is carried by `iclr2026_conference.sty`, the style that the ICLR organisation ships in its own Master-Template repository (manifest source `pub`), loaded on `article` (iclr2026_conference.tex:2-3); under the rubric's official-kit ruling it is published (W1) and is one class-like way, `class.iclr2026_conference`, with one boolean switch `\iclrfinalcopy` (W2, iclr2026_conference.sty:29-31). Everything the sample adds is a published package, a kernel command or a reducible alias, and there is no artwork and no external tool (BibTeX is the ordinary toolchain). The yes rests on that ruling: if the style were zip-local, the left line-number ruler (sty:195-240, a counter-driven `\loop` box builder with no lib way) would be `BESPOKE/class` and the redefined `\maketitle` block (sty:61-102) would be a close call. Both are listed under Bespoke code so the verdict can be re-read.

## Blockers
none

## Class setting
- Class: `class.article` options: none (iclr2026_conference.tex:2). House style `class.iclr2026_conference` (class-like, as the lib treats a house style), loaded by `\usepackage{iclr2026_conference,times}` (tex:3) with no options; its only switch is `\iclrfinalcopy` (sty:29-31), commented out at tex:49, so the anonymous submission mode is on.
- Class-native vocabulary used: `\title` and `\author` with `\\`, `\thanks` and the separators `\And` and `\AND` (tex:12, 18-36, 25, 31; defined sty:89-99), `\maketitle` (tex:53), the restyled `abstract` environment (tex:55-61; sty:104-105), `\section` to `\subsubsection` and `\subsubsection*` (tex:63, 76, 146, 397, 401), natbib `\citet` `\citep` `\cite` (tex:163-165, 231), `\bibliographystyle{iclr2026_conference}` (tex:407), `\appendix` (tex:409). Default mode prints "Anonymous authors / Paper under double-blind review" instead of the author block (sty:100), the head "Under review as a conference paper at ICLR 2026" (sty:95) and the left ruler (sty:230-240); `\iclrfinalcopy` switches all three (sty:87-93, 231).
