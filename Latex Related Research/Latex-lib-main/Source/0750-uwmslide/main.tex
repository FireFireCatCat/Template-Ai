\begin{slide}{Motivation}
Isolated DC-DC converters used in the lab's test rigs currently top out at
88\% efficiency at rated load. This update covers a revised transformer
winding that targets 94\% while keeping the same footprint.
\end{slide}

\begin{doubleslide}{Converter Topology}
The revised design keeps the full-bridge primary side unchanged and
replaces the secondary winding with an interleaved layout.

\[
\eta = \frac{P_{\mathrm{out}}}{P_{\mathrm{out}} + P_{\mathrm{loss}}}
\]

Interleaving halves the RMS current per winding segment, which is where
most of the loss reduction comes from.
\slidedivider
\begin{tabular}{lr}
Primary turns & 12 \\
Secondary turns & 6 \\
Core & ETD49 \\
Switching freq. & 100 kHz
\end{tabular}
\end{doubleslide}

\begin{leftitem}{Bench Setup}
\item Electronic load swept 10--100\% rated current
\item Efficiency logged at 20 points per sweep
\item Thermal camera on the transformer body
\slidedivider
Full-load thermal images show a peak core temperature of 61\textdegree C,
down from 79\textdegree C on the original winding.
\end{leftitem}

\begin{rightitem}{Efficiency Curve}
Efficiency rises steadily from light load and plateaus above 40\% rated
current, where interleaving pays off most.
\slidedivider
\item 20\% load: 89.1\%
\item 50\% load: 93.8\%
\item 80\% load: 94.2\%
\item 100\% load: 93.6\%
\end{rightitem}

\begin{doubleitem}{Comparison}
\item Original winding
\item Peak efficiency 88.3\%
\item Peak core temp 79\textdegree C
\slidedivider
\item Interleaved winding
\item Peak efficiency 94.2\%
\item Peak core temp 61\textdegree C
\end{doubleitem}
