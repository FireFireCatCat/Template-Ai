\simpleRecipe{Crispy chickpea salad}{Serves 2}{\begin{itemize}\item 1 can chickpeas\item 1 cucumber\item 2 tomatoes\item olive oil\item lemon and herbs\end{itemize}}{Dry the chickpeas and roast with oil and seasoning at 200\textdegree C for 25 minutes. \simpleRecipe{One-pan apple oats}{Serves 2}{\begin{itemize}\item 2 apples\item 100 g oats\item 250 ml milk\item cinnamon\item 1 tbsp maple syrup\end{itemize}}{Dice the apples into a saucepan. Add oats, milk, and cinnamon and simmer gently until creamy. Finish with maple syrup and serve warm.}
\simpleRecipe{Herbed flatbread}{Makes 4}{\begin{itemize}\item 250 g flour\item 150 ml water\item 1 tbsp olive oil\item salt\item fresh herbs\end{itemize}}{Mix flour, water, oil, and salt into a soft dough.
