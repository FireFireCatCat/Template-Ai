\subsection*{Mathematics}

\LaTeX{} is well suited to typesetting mathematics. Let $X_1, X_2, \ldots,
X_n$ be independent and identically distributed with $\text{E}[X_i] = \mu$
and $\text{Var}[X_i] = \sigma^2 < \infty$, and let
$$S_n = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i}^{n} X_i$$
denote their mean. As $n \to \infty$, $\sqrt{n}(S_n - \mu)$ converges in
distribution to $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$.
