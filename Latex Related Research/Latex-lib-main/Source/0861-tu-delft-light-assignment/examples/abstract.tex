This report presents the static strength and fatigue-life verification of an
aluminium L-bracket that cantilevers a vibration sensor from a test-rig
frame. A free-body analysis is used to derive the root bending moment and
shear force from the specified tip load, from which the peak bending stress
and the corresponding factor of safety are computed. A stress-concentration
factor at the mounting-hole fillet is included and the resulting local stress
is used with a Goodman diagram to estimate the fatigue life under the
specified cyclic load. The bracket is shown to satisfy the static margin
requirement but only marginally satisfies the fatigue-life requirement,
motivating a fillet-radius increase discussed in the recommendations.
