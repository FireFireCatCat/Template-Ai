\noindent{\Large\bfseries Miso Butter Soba Noodles}

\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent\begin{tikzpicture}
  \draw[tagline, line width=1.2pt] (0,0) -- (\linewidth,0);

\vspace{0.8em}

\begin{multicols}{2}

{\color{tagline}\bfseries\small INGREDIENTS}
\vspace{0.3em}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1em, itemsep=2pt, label={\color{tagline}\tiny\textbullet}]
  \item 200\,g dried soba noodles
  \item 2 tbsp white miso paste
  \item 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  \item 1 tbsp soy sauce
  \item 1 tsp rice vinegar
  \item 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  \item 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
  \item 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  \item 1 soft-boiled egg per serving (optional)
  \item Chilli oil, to taste
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}
{\color{tagline}\bfseries\small NUTRITION (per serving)}
\vspace{0.3em}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1em, itemsep=1pt, label={\color{tagline}\tiny\textbullet}]
  \item 420 kcal
  \item 14g protein
  \item 12g fat
  \item 62g carbohydrate
\end{itemize}

\columnbreak

{\color{tagline}\bfseries\small METHOD}
\vspace{0.3em}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.3em, itemsep=5pt,
                   label={\color{tagline}\bfseries\arabic*}]
  \item Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the soba noodles
    according to the package instructions, usually 4--5 minutes.
  \item While the noodles cook, whisk together the miso paste, soy sauce,
    rice vinegar and sesame oil in a small bowl until smooth.
  \item Drain the noodles, reserving 3 tbsp of the starchy cooking water.
  \item Return the empty pot to low heat and melt the butter. Add the miso
    mixture and the reserved cooking water, stirring to combine into a
    smooth sauce.
  \item Add the drained noodles back to the pot and toss to coat evenly in
    the sauce, using tongs to separate any clumps.
  \item Divide between bowls. Top with spring onions, sesame seeds, and a
    soft-boiled egg if using.
  \item Finish with a drizzle of chilli oil to taste and serve
    immediately.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.8em}
{\color{tagline}\bfseries\small SWAP IT}
\vspace{0.3em}

Out of soba? Udon or even spaghetti work in a pinch --- the miso butter
sauce is forgiving of whatever noodle you have on hand.
