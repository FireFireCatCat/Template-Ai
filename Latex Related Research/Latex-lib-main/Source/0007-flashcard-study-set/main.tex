\flashcardtopic{Field methods}
\begin{document}
\flashcard{What is a control variable?}{A factor held constant so that the effect of the variable under study can be isolated.}
\flashcard{What does a confidence interval describe?}{A range produced by a method that captures the parameter at a stated long-run rate.}
\flashcard[Review]{What is a falsifiable hypothesis?}{A claim that an observation or experiment could show to be false.}
\flashcard{Why randomise treatment assignment?}{To reduce systematic differences between groups before treatment begins.}
\flashcard{What is a reproducible analysis?}{An analysis another person can rerun from recorded inputs and obtain the same result.}
\flashcard{What belongs in a method section?}{Enough procedural detail for a reader to understand and repeat the study.}
\flashcard{What is a confounder?}{A factor associated with both the exposure and the outcome that can distort their apparent relationship.}
\flashcard{What is a residual?}{The observed response minus the response predicted by a fitted model.}
