\section*{Part A --- Short Answer}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item State the octet rule in one sentence.

  \item Classify the bond formed between sodium and chlorine in NaCl as
    ionic, covalent, or metallic.

  \item Draw the Lewis structure of carbon dioxide, CO$_2$.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Part B --- Extended Response}

\begin{enumerate}[resume,label=\arabic*.]
  \item Explain, with reference to electronegativity, why the H--F bond
    in hydrogen fluoride is polar covalent while the H--H bond in
    hydrogen gas is nonpolar covalent.

  \item A student claims that all ionic compounds conduct electricity in
    the solid state. Explain why this claim is incorrect, and describe
    the condition under which an ionic compound \emph{does} conduct
    electricity.
\end{enumerate}
