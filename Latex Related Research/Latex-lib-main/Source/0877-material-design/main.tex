\section{Why Cards}

\begin{frame}{A flat-card layout}
  \begin{card}
    Every piece of content lives inside a card: a flat rectangle with a soft
    drop shadow, following Google's Material Design guidelines rather than
    Beamer's usual frame-and-block conventions.
  \end{card}
  \begin{card}[Titled card]
    Cards can carry their own title bar, separate from the frame title.
  \end{card}
\end{frame}
