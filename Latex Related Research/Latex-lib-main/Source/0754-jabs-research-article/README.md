# JABS research article starter

Original neutral manuscript starter reflecting the University of Zambia Journal of Agricultural and Biomedical Sciences announced LaTeX workflow. It includes structured abstract, field methods, statistics, results, discussion, and declarations.

Source: https://graduate.unza.zm/research/projects/diamond-open-access/news-and-updates/46-jabs-launches-official-author-templates-in-microsoft-word-and-latex

The announcement confirms an official LaTeX download but does not state a redistribution licence. Upstream class, logos, and assets are not bundled.
