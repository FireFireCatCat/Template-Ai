\chapter{Related Work}
\label{ch:related}

\section{Deep Speaker Embeddings}
X-vector embeddings, trained with a classification objective over speaker
identity and pooled across an utterance, remain a strong baseline for
speaker verification \citep{snyder2018}.

\section{Channel and Domain Robustness}
Joint factor analysis and related generative approaches explicitly model
channel variability as a separate latent factor from speaker identity,
predating the deep embedding era but sharing the same underlying goal
\citep{kenny2010}.

\section{Gap Addressed by This Thesis}
Most channel-robustness work for deep embeddings relies on large amounts
of unpaired multi-channel data and domain adversarial training. This
thesis explores a paired-data setting, where a modest number of speakers
have recordings across multiple channels, which allows more direct
supervision of what should and should not change across channels.
