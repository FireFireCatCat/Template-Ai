\chapter{Foreword}

I first met Nadia Farouk at a reading she gave in a bookshop that no
longer exists. She read the opening pages of what would become this
book, and afterward I asked her how long the character had been living
in her head. "Since I was twelve," she said, "and I've been trying to
get her to stop talking ever since." The stories that follow are the
result of that argument.
