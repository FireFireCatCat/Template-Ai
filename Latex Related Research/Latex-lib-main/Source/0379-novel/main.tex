\begin{ChapterStart}

\ChapterTitle{--- 1 ---}
\end{ChapterStart}
Sofia Bellini had inherited three things from her father: his stubborn
jaw, his workshop on the Via degli Orologi, and a clock that would not
keep time no matter how many times she took it apart.

She turned it over in her hands one more time before setting it back
on the bench. Outside, Trieste was waking up in its usual unhurried
way, fishing boats easing out past the breakwater, the smell of
roasting coffee drifting up from the café below.

\scenebreak

The letter had arrived that morning, sealed with wax the colour of
dried blood. She recognized the crest before she broke it: the
Contessa's household, the one that had not written to her family in
eleven years.

\begin{ChapterStart}
\vspace*{2\nbs}
\ChapterTitle{--- 2 ---}
\end{ChapterStart}
The villa on the hill had not changed. Sofia remembered the gate, the
gravel drive, the particular green of the cypress trees against a sky
that never seemed to make up its mind about rain.

What had changed was the silence. No dogs barking. No servants in the
courtyard. Just a single clock, visible through a ground-floor window,
its pendulum frozen mid-swing.
