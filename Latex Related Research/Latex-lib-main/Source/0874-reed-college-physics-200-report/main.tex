\section{Introduction}
We measured the oscillation period of a mass--spring system and used the result
to estimate its effective spring constant. A useful model is
\begin{equation}
  x(t)=A\,e^{-\gamma t}\cos(\omega t+\phi).
\end{equation}
\section{Method}
The mass was displaced by approximately \(3\,\mathrm{cm}\), released without
an impulse, and recorded for ten cycles. The equilibrium position was checked
before each trial.

decrease in amplitude indicates weak damping. Discuss systematic effects,
