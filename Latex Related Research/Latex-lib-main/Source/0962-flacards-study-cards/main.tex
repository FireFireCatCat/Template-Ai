\card{Define sampling bias.}{A systematic difference between the target population and the observations actually collected.}
\card{What is a median?}{The middle value after the observations are put in order.}
\card{What does correlation measure?}{The direction and strength of a specified association, not causation by itself.}
\card{Define an independent variable.}{A variable whose change is used to explain or predict a response.}
\card{What is a null hypothesis?}{A reference claim, usually of no effect or no difference, tested against an alternative.}
\card{Why record units?}{Units make numerical quantities interpretable and prevent silent scale errors.}
\card{What is a confidence level?}{The long-run coverage rate of a confidence-interval procedure under repeated sampling.}
\card{What is an outlier?}{An observation unusually far from the pattern represented by the rest of the data.}
