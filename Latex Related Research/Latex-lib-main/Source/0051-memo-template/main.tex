\section*{Purpose}
This memo proposes a small pilot for the neighbourhood data dashboard. The
pilot will test whether a weekly, plain-language summary helps community
partners find the measures most relevant to local planning.
\section*{Proposed work}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Confirm the three participating partner organisations.
  \item Publish a private preview with anonymised weekly totals.
  \item Hold a 45-minute feedback session and record requested changes.
  \item Decide whether to expand the dashboard after the four-week pilot.
\end{enumerate}
\section*{Decision requested}
Please approve the pilot scope and nominate one contact from each partner by
5 September. The project team will circulate the first preview two working
days later.
