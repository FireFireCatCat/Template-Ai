\section{Problem Statement}

A simply supported beam of length $L = 4\,\text{m}$ carries a uniformly
distributed load $w = 12\,\text{kN/m}$ over its entire span. Determine
the maximum bending moment and the maximum deflection, given a flexural
rigidity $EI = 5.2 \times 10^{6}\,\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2$.

\section{Reaction Forces}

By symmetry, each support carries half the total load:
\[
  R_A = R_B = \frac{wL}{2} = \frac{12 \times 4}{2} = 24\,\text{kN}.
\]

\section{Maximum Bending Moment}

For a simply supported beam under a uniformly distributed load, the
maximum bending moment occurs at midspan:
\[
  M_{\max} = \frac{wL^2}{8} = \frac{12 \times 4^2}{8} = 24\,\text{kN}\cdot\text{m}.
\]

\section{Maximum Deflection}

The maximum deflection of a simply supported beam under a uniformly
distributed load also occurs at midspan:
\[
  \delta_{\max} = \frac{5wL^4}{384\,EI}
  = \frac{5 \times 12{,}000 \times 4^4}{384 \times 5.2 \times 10^{6}}
  \approx 0.0154\,\text{m} = 15.4\,\text{mm}.
\]

\section{Conclusion}

The beam experiences a maximum bending moment of $24\,\text{kN}\cdot
\text{m}$ and a maximum deflection of approximately $15.4\,\text{mm}$ at
midspan. Both values should be checked against the allowable stress and
deflection limits specified in the applicable design code before the
beam section is finalised.
