# Wireless Foundations poster

A 40in × 30in landscape poster starter based on the
[Wireless Foundations conference poster template](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/latex-template-for-wireless-foundations-conference-posters/tnqfxysjzyqt),
originally published on ShareLaTeX and attributed to Anand D. Sarwate after
modification of Ron Kumon's layout.

The original's defining mechanism is manual placement on a coarse grid. This
starter retains that idea using `textpos` blocks and replaces EPS logos,
PostScript-only decoration, and personal contact details with vector TikZ and
fictional content. Coordinates are in centimetres on the 40in × 30in canvas.

Overleaf labels the upstream item “Other (as stated in the work)” and no
separate permissive grant was located in the reviewed page. It is therefore a
promoted provenance/licence warning under the catalogue policy. No Wireless
Foundations, Berkeley, or other institutional marks are bundled.
