# Minimalist Invoice

An uncluttered A4 invoice starter for a small consultancy or studio. The
example demonstrates a compact sender/recipient header, a readable item table,
payment terms, and a clear total without relying on a custom class.

Compile with PDFLaTeX.

Upstream reference: https://gitlab.com/david-alvarez-rosa/minimalist-invoice

The upstream licence and bundled-asset provenance were not exposed during the
catalogue review. This catalogue starter is newly written and does not bundle
upstream source, logos, fonts, or customer data; review the upstream grant
before redistributing modifications to the original project.
