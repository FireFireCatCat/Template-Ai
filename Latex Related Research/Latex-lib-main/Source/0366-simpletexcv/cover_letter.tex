\opening{Dear Hiring Team,}

I am writing to apply for the Senior Backend Engineer role at Meridian
Labs. In my current position at Northwind Systems I led the migration of
our order-processing service to a queue-based architecture, cutting p99
latency by 40\% and removing a recurring source of on-call pages.

Before that, at Fernbridge Analytics, I built the ingestion pipeline
that now handles two million events a day. Along the way I picked up a
few habits I would bring to your team:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Instrumenting a service before it needs to page anyone, not after.
    \item Writing runbooks that a teammate can follow at 3am without calling me.
    \item Treating a migration's rollback plan as part of the design, not an afterthought.
\end{itemize}

I am drawn to Meridian's work on real-time data infrastructure and would
welcome the chance to bring that same focus on reliability to your team.
