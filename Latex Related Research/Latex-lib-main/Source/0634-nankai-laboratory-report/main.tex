\section{Purpose}
Determine the concentration of an unknown monoprotic acid by titration with a
standard sodium hydroxide solution and assess repeatability.

\section{Background}
At the equivalence point, the stoichiometric relation is
\[
  c_\mathrm{acid}V_\mathrm{acid}=c_\mathrm{base}V_\mathrm{base}.
\]
The endpoint is identified from the indicator colour change. Record whether
the final drop was added slowly enough to avoid overshooting.

\section{Materials and procedure}
List the burette, pipette, conical flask, indicator, standard solution and
unknown. Rinse the burette with titrant, perform one rough run, then repeat
until two concordant titres are available.
