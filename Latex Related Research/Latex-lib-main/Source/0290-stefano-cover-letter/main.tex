\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Senior Structural Engineer position
advertised on your careers page. I have spent the past six years
designing pedestrian and light-rail bridges for Southern Cross
Infrastructure, most recently leading the structural design for the
Wynyard Point pedestrian bridge, a 140-metre cable-stayed span that
opened eight months ahead of schedule.

What draws me to Delta Bridgeworks specifically is your recent work on
seismic retrofits for heritage-listed structures --- a problem that
combines the structural rigour I enjoy with the constraint-driven
creativity of working within a protected facade. I have led two similar
retrofit projects, both requiring close collaboration with heritage
architects to reconcile code compliance with preservation requirements.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience in
long-span and retrofit design could contribute to your upcoming projects.
I have attached my CV and portfolio, and I am available for an interview
at your convenience.
