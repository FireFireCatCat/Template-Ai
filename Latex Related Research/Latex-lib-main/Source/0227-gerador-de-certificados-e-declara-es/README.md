# Gerador de Certificados e Declarações

This self-contained starter demonstrates a printable Portuguese certificate
and declaration layout. Replace the example names, dates, wording, and
signatures with your own content.

The research lead is the Overleaf gallery entry
<https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/gerador-de-certificados-e-declaracoes/vznwmscnzqyb>.
The upstream licence and asset provenance were not stated in the reviewed
material, so this catalogue starter includes no upstream code, logo, or font.
