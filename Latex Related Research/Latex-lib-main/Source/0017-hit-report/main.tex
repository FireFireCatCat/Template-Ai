\begin{abstract}
This report measures the viscosity of a transparent liquid from the terminal
speed of a small sphere. The worked structure separates observations from
interpretation and leaves clear places for repeat measurements and uncertainty
budgets.
\end{abstract}

\section{Objective}
Estimate the dynamic viscosity $\eta$ and compare it with a reference value
after accounting for timing and diameter uncertainties.

\section{Principle and method}
At terminal speed, the viscous drag balances the effective weight. For a sphere
of radius $r$ and density $\rho_s$ falling through a liquid of density $\rho_l$,
\begin{equation}
  \eta = \frac{2r^2g(\rho_s-\rho_l)}{9v_t}.
\end{equation}
Measure the sphere diameter at several orientations, release it without a push,
and time its passage between two marks after the speed has stabilised.
\citep{young2024measurement}
