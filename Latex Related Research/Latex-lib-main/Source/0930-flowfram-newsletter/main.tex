{\sffamily\Large\bfseries The two-week test\par}
\smallskip
\noindent\textit{A compact report designed for the space between a bulletin
and a magazine feature.}

\medskip
Volunteers placed a movable shade marker at three waiting areas and recorded
arrival time, approximate queue length, and whether people stayed or walked on.
The measure is modest by design: it supports a next decision without claiming
to be a complete evaluation.

\medskip
\textbf{What we noticed.}
People paused longest where shade and a seat were separated by more than a few
steps. A clear route marker prompted more questions than a dense map. The
busiest crossing was also the place where the queue moved slowest.

\medskip
\textbf{The next experiment.}
Repeat the observation after rain. Bring one unanswered question and one idea
small enough to try before the following issue. The frame definitions above
can be adjusted without changing the article content.
