\chapter{A Clear Beginning}
Every book has a promise to its reader. State that promise early, then give each chapter one job. A strong structure is easier to revise when the source mirrors the finished work.
\section{The shape of a chapter}
Use sections sparingly and reserve them for meaningful changes of subject. Lists, quotations, and small tables can break up an argument without turning the page into a dashboard.

\item Check the table of contents and running heads after revision.
