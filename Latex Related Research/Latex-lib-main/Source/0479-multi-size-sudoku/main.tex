\section*{How to use this sheet}
Fill each grid so that a value appears only once in every row, column, and
sub-grid.  The `multi-sudoku` package accepts familiar tabular syntax and
supports grids larger than the usual 9 by 9 format.

\begin{center}
  \begin{minipage}[t]{0.31\linewidth}
    \centering
    \textcolor{puzzleblue}{\large\bfseries 4 $\times$ 4}\par\smallskip
    \begin{sudoku}[size=4x4,length=2.1em,fontsize=\normalsize,thickness=1pt]
      1 &   & 3 &   \NL
        & 4 &   & 2 \nl
        & 1 & 4 &   \NL
      4 &   &   & 1
    \end{sudoku}
  \end{minipage}\hfill
  \begin{minipage}[t]{0.64\linewidth}
    \centering
    \textcolor{puzzleblue}{\large\bfseries 9 $\times$ 9}\par\smallskip
    \begin{sudoku}[size=9x9,length=1.17em,fontsize=\scriptsize,thickness=1pt]
      5 & 3 &   &   & 7 &   &   &   &   \nl
      6 &   &   & 1 & 9 & 5 &   &   &   \nl
        & 9 & 8 &   &   &   &   & 6 &   \NL
      8 &   &   &   & 6 &   &   &   & 3 \nl
      4 &   &   & 8 &   & 3 &   &   & 1 \nl
      7 &   &   &   & 2 &   &   &   & 6 \NL
        & 6 &   &   &   &   & 2 & 8 &   \nl
        &   &   & 4 & 1 & 9 &   &   & 5 \nl
        &   &   &   & 8 &   &   & 7 & 9
    \end{sudoku}
  \end{minipage}
\end{center}

\section*{Editing notes}
Change the optional `size`, `length`, `fontsize`, and `thickness` keys on an
individual `sudoku` environment to make a worksheet or a large-format puzzle.
Blank cells are left empty; clues are ordinary numbers.
