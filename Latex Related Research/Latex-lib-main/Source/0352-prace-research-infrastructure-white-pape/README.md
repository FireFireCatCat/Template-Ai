# Research Infrastructure White Paper

An original consortium-style white-paper starter for a scientific case,
service model, work plan, governance, sustainability, and partner decision.
It follows the subject area of the [PRACE white-paper source](https://repository.prace-ri.eu/git/latex-templates/prace-whitepaper)
without bundling upstream source, PRACE branding, logos, or text.

Upstream licence/provenance was not established in the catalogue research;
review the current source before redistribution or derivative use. Compile with
pdfLaTeX.
