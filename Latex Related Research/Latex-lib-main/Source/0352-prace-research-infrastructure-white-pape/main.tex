\section*{Executive summary}
Summarise the scientific or engineering need, the shared infrastructure
proposed, the benefit to users, and the decision required from partners.
\section{Scientific case}
Describe the research questions and explain why coordination, shared tooling,
or shared compute is needed. Identify the communities and use cases served.
\section{Service model}

\section{Work plan and governance}
\begin{enumerate}\item Agree common requirements and interfaces.\item Validate
the service with representative users.\item Publish results, costs, and
lessons learned for the next funding or review cycle.\end{enumerate}
\section{Risks, sustainability and impact}
Record technical, financial, staffing, security, and community risks. Explain
how the service will remain useful, maintainable, and accessible after the
initial project ends.
\section{Partner decision}
List the commitments requested from each partner, the approval date, and the
first checkpoint at which progress will be reviewed.
