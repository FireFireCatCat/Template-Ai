\section*{New Safety Protocol Rollout}

\lettrine[lines=2]{S}{tarting} 1 October, all floor staff will follow the
updated lockout/tagout procedure for the stamping line, following last
quarter's near-miss review. The revised checklist adds a second sign-off step
before any guard is removed for maintenance.

Training sessions run three times daily during the first week of October ---
6:30am, 2:30pm and 10:30pm --- to cover all three shifts. Attendance is
mandatory; supervisors will track sign-in sheets at each session.

\begin{sidebox}{Quick Reference}
  \begin{itemize}
    \setlength\itemsep{2pt}
    \item New checklist posted at every stamping station
    \item Second sign-off required before guard removal
    \item Questions: safety@ridgeline-mfg.example
  \end{itemize}
\end{sidebox}

Floor supervisors received the full procedure document last week. If you
have not seen it, ask your shift lead before the training session --- the
updated forms will not be accepted until everyone has reviewed the changes.

\section*{Employee Spotlight: Maria Chen}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=gray!8, colframe=gray!40, boxrule=0.5pt,
                   left=6pt, right=6pt, top=4pt, bottom=4pt]
  \textit{``I started on the floor eight years ago and never expected to end
  up running the whole night shift. The thing that's kept me here is that
  people actually listen when you flag a problem.''}
\end{tcolorbox}

Maria Chen joined Ridgeline in 2018 as a line operator and was promoted to
Night Shift Supervisor this spring. Under her watch, the night shift posted
its lowest defect rate in company history last quarter --- a 2.1\% drop from
the prior year.

Maria credits the improvement to a simple change: a five-minute handoff
huddle at the start of each shift where operators flag anything unusual from
the shift before. ``It sounds too simple to matter,'' she says, ``but it
means problems get caught in hour one instead of hour six.''

Congratulations, Maria, on a well-earned promotion.
