\chapter{Family favourites}
\recipe{Sunday Tomato Sauce}{Makes 6 cups}{Prep 15 min; simmer 45 min}{\begin{itemize}\item 2 kg ripe tomatoes\item 2 onions\item 4 garlic cloves\item olive oil\item basil and salt\end{itemize}}{Soften the onions in olive oil, then add garlic and tomatoes. Simmer uncovered until thick and glossy. Season with salt, tear in basil, and preserve or serve over pasta.}
\recipe{Maple Oat Cookies}{Makes 18}{Prep 15 min; bake 12 min}{\begin{itemize}\item 120 g rolled oats\item 100 g flour\item 90 g butter\item 80 g maple syrup\item 1 egg\end{itemize}}{Heat the oven to 180\textdegree C. Cream the butter and syrup, beat in the egg, and fold in the oats and flour. Spoon onto a tray and bake until the edges are golden.}
