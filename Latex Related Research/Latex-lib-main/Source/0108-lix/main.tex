\title{On the Configurability of Long Documents}
\subtitle{A Worked Example}
\author{Noor Fatima}
\keywords{LaTeX, thesis, typesetting}
\abstract{This thesis argues that a single, well-structured document class
can carry the full weight of long-form academic writing, from a title page
through a compiled bibliography. We demonstrate the approach with a worked
section, a cited source, and a compiled bibliography.}

\subsection{Motivation}
Long-document classes tend to specialise early, one per institution or
publisher. \cite{knuth1984texbook} shows that the underlying typesetting
engine does not require this — the constraints come from convention, not
from \TeX{} itself.
