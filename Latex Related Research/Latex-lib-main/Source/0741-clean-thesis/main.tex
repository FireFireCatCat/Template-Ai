\chapter*{Abstract}
This thesis argues that a clean, distraction-free thesis style can carry the
same structural rigour as a full document class. We demonstrate the approach
with a worked chapter, a cited source, and a compiled bibliography.

\section{Motivation}
Thesis styles are often bundled with heavyweight document classes. This
starter shows that a style package layered on a standard class, such as
\texttt{scrreprt}, is enough to produce a clean, well-structured document, as
argued by \textcite{knuth1984texbook}.
