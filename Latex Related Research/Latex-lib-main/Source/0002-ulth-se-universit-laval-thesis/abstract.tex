\chapter*{Abstract}
\label{chap-abstract}
\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{\nameref{chap-abstract}}

\begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
Environmental sensor networks often sample at a fixed interval, which
wastes energy when monitored conditions change slowly. This thesis proposes
an adaptive sampling strategy that adjusts measurement frequency based on
recent signal variability, extending battery life without a noticeable
loss of data fidelity. We evaluate the strategy on three deployed sensor
networks and show that it reduces energy consumption substantially relative
to fixed-interval sampling, at a comparable reconstruction error.
\end{otherlanguage*}
