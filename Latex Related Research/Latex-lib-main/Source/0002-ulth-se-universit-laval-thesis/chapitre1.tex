\section{Fixed and Adaptive Sampling}
Classical sampling theory specifies a fixed rate sufficient to reconstruct
a signal of known bandwidth, but real environmental signals rarely have a
single, known bandwidth throughout a deployment \citep{Jain2004}.

\section{Energy-Aware Sensing}
Energy-aware sensing schemes trade measurement fidelity for battery life,
often by adapting either the sampling rate or the transmission rate based
on observed conditions \citep{Alippi2008}.

\section{Gap Addressed by This Thesis}
Prior adaptive sampling work has largely been evaluated in simulation.
This thesis evaluates an adaptive sampling strategy on real long-term
field deployments, where sensor drift and environmental noise are present
in ways simulation does not capture.
\end{otherlanguage*}
