\noindent\textbf{Parties:} \emph{Your Organisation} (the Client) and
\emph{Your Supplier} (the Supplier). This fictional starter demonstrates
structured, numbered clauses; replace all names and terms with advice from a
qualified professional.

\begin{contract}
\Clause[title={Purpose and scope}]
The Supplier will provide the services described in the project brief. The
parties may replace this clause with a precise statement of work.

\SubClause[title={Deliverables}]
The Supplier will deliver the agreed materials in the format and on the dates
listed in the project schedule.

\Clause[title={Fees and payment}]
The Client will pay the fees stated in the project schedule within thirty days
of a correctly issued invoice.

\Clause[title={Confidentiality}]
Each party will protect confidential information received from the other party
and will use it only for performing this agreement.

\Clause[title={Term and termination}]
This agreement begins on the effective date and ends when the deliverables are
accepted, unless either party terminates it under the agreed notice terms.
\end{contract}
