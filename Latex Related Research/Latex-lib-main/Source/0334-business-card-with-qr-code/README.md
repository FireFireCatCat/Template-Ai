# businesscard-qrcode

A printer-ready single visiting card with an encoded vCard QR code. The
starter exercises the CTAN class's exact paper/content dimensions, crop marks,
privacy-conscious contact fields, and XeLaTeX workflow. Replace the clearly
marked sample identity before printing and test the QR code on the final PDF.

`businesscard-qrcode` is distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public
License and is supplied by TeX Live; the class is not bundled in this archive.
No logos or third-party artwork are included.
