\noindent{\large\bfseries Farmers' Market Returns to the Green Every Saturday}

\medskip
\noindent
After a two-year hiatus, the Maple Street farmers' market is back on the
village green every Saturday morning from 8am to noon, running through the
end of October. Eleven vendors have signed on so far, selling produce,
baked goods, honey and handmade soap.

\medskip
\noindent\textit{``We've wanted this back since it closed during the road
works,''} says organiser Priya Nair. \textit{``Having it on the green again,
right where people already walk their dogs, makes it feel like the market
never left.''}

\bigskip
\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}
\bigskip

\section*{\normalsize Road Resurfacing Schedule}

The town has confirmed the resurfacing schedule for Maple Street and the
three side streets, running in phases to keep at least one lane open at all
times.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Week 1} --- Maple St, north end (Elm to 3rd)
  \item \textbf{Week 2} --- Maple St, south end (3rd to Birch)
  \item \textbf{Week 3} --- Sycamore Lane
  \item \textbf{Week 4} --- Willow Court
\end{enumerate}

Residents on affected blocks will get a door-hanger notice 48 hours before
work begins. Street parking will be suspended on work days only.
