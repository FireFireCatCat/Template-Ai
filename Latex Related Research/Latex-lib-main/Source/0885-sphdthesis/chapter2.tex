\chapter{Related Work}
Consensus protocols have a long history in distributed systems research, from
early work on the Byzantine Generals problem \cite{Lamport1982} through
leader-based protocols designed for practical adoption \cite{Ongaro2014}.

\section{Synchronous and Partially Synchronous Models}
Most deployed consensus protocols assume partial synchrony: message delays are
bounded, but the bound is unknown to participants \cite{Fischer1985}. This
assumption underlies both Paxos-family protocols and Raft.

\section{Gaps Addressed by This Thesis}
Prior evaluations of consensus protocols under loss typically treat loss as a
binary partition event, rather than a continuous parameter. A later evaluation
chapter (omitted from this starter) would test the proposed protocol across a
continuous range of loss rates.
