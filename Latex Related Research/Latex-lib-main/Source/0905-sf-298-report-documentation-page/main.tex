\Abstract{This report documents a practical handover method for field systems.
The method combines a readiness review, an explicit decision record, a recovery
exercise, and a signed acceptance record. A six-month pilot reduced the median
time to assign an owner from 31 hours to 9 hours. The report records the method,
its operating measures, and the limitations that should be considered before
adopting it elsewhere.}
