# Pilot, rubric v0 and lib v0

29 zips analysed once (`solutions/`) and 8 of them again, independently (`second-opinions/`),
before the rubric was tightened. Verdicts agreed on all 8 pairs; the sets of ways used
overlapped 65 to 94% (one pair 0%: one analyst listed a class-only zip as one way, the
other decomposed it). Kept as calibration data. These zips are analysed again under the
final rubric and the results are in `Solution/`; nothing here counts toward the totals.
What changed afterwards: a publisher's official kit counts as published, blockers carry a
scope (class, preamble, body, package), two header fields were added (`Look carried by`,
`Class origin`), and class ways need no proposal.
